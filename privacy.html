Privacy Policy

How SBTech Consulting collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: June 2, 2026

SBTech Consulting ("SBTech," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website at sbtechma.com (the "Site") and in the course of providing our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to respond to you and provide our services:

  • Information you provide. When you submit our contact form or email us, we collect your name, email address, phone number (if provided), and the contents of your message.
  • Client information. When you engage us for services, we may collect information necessary to deliver those services, such as business contact details and technical information about your systems.
  • Automatically collected information. Our hosting provider and any analytics tools may collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and the date/time of your visit.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and communicate with you.
  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services.
  • To send service-related information you have requested.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

3. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We maintain strict professional confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate the Site and our business (for example, our website host and contact-form provider), and only to the extent necessary, or when required by law.

4. Contact Form & Third-Party Services

Our contact form is processed by a third-party form-delivery service that forwards submissions to our email. Our Site may also use a content delivery network for web fonts and is hosted by a third-party hosting provider. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms.

5. Data Security

We use secure, encrypted communication channels and reasonable administrative and technical safeguards — including multi-factor authentication and encrypted remote management tools — to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information consistent with industry standards.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Your Choices

You may request that we access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at the details below. You can also opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at info@sbtechma.com or (413) 813-9224, SBTech Consulting, Palmer, MA.

This policy is provided as general information and is not legal advice. We recommend having it reviewed by a qualified attorney to ensure it meets your specific legal and regulatory obligations.
