# SBTech Consulting — LLM.txt

## Company Overview
SBTech Consulting is an independent IT consulting firm based in Palmer, MA, founded in 2014.
The business is operated by owner Joseph P. Nompleggi and provides vendor-neutral technology
services to small businesses. Previously registered as an LLC, the company voluntarily dissolved
that entity in 2025 to operate as a lean independent consultancy with no active legal or
financial encumbrances.

## What We Do
We are a small business dedicated to helping other small businesses implement technology
effectively. We specialize in customizing affordable and efficient IT support solutions,
including managed services.

## Services
- Hardware Procurement and Computer Management
- Software Licensing
- Comprehensive Training
- Networking and Installation
- Security Audits and Monitoring
- Patching, Repairs, and Virus Removal
- Troubleshooting and Break/Fix Solutions
- Managed Services
- Website Design

## Areas of Expertise
- Technology Consulting — system integration, software development, IT support
- Operations Management — process optimization and efficiency enhancement
- Project Management — oversight of IT and website design projects
- Data Analytics — data-driven insights for informed decision-making
- Change Management — organizational transitions and technology integration

## Differentiators
- Vendor-Agnostic IT Support: Unbiased technology guidance without vendor commissions or
  software agendas.
- System optimization, automation, and AI integration.
- Core IT infrastructure support including networking and cloud solutions.

## Compliance & Security
We maintain strict professional confidentiality, never sell data, and use secure, encrypted
communication channels. Security measures include multi-factor authentication and encrypted
remote management tools aligned with industry standards.
