About SBTech

Independent, honest IT guidance

SBTech Consulting is an independent IT consulting firm based in Palmer, MA, founded in 2014 by Joseph P. Nompleggi. We provide vendor-neutral technology services to small businesses — unbiased guidance without vendor commissions or software agendas.

We maintain strict professional confidentiality, never sell data, and use secure, encrypted communication channels. Security measures include multi-factor authentication and encrypted remote management tools aligned with industry standards.
